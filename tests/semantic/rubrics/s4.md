# S4: the trace read without the agent, judged

A reader who did not watch the run was given only the `kapso trace` output and the campaign directory, and answered six questions (docs/plans/semantic-production-tests.md, section 5). You hold the fact sheet, extracted from the record and checked; the reader never saw it. Score each answer 0 or 1:

- **what won**: 1 when the reader names the delivered branch and its score as the fact sheet has them.
- **why**: 1 when every fact the reader cites checks out against the fact sheet and the conclusion names its evidence; the reasoning may differ from the sheet's, the facts may not.
- **what was voided and on what evidence**: 1 when the voided attempts and their evidence match the fact sheet (an answer of "nothing voided" is right only when the sheet says so).
- **what was rejected and why**: 1 when the rejected candidates and their reasons match the fact sheet.
- **what it cost**: 1 when the cost and its coverage match the fact sheet.
- **safe to merge**: 1 when every fact the reader cites checks out and the conclusion names its evidence; "not established" is right where the fact sheet says the record does not settle it.
