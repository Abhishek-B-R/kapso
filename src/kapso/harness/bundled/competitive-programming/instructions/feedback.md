# Feedback

Weigh beside the score:

- the score and the runtime against the baseline, the current best or the
  board, per seed and overall; headroom left unspent under the time limit
  is a finding, since a contest rewards the time used;
- which family the candidate belongs to and whether that family is
  saturated with evidence (the heuristic-patterns skill names the
  families): a third annealer with new moves is not a new family;
- the category of cases where the candidate loses, never a single case;
- the economics (the contest-economics skill): the time to a first strong
  score, the critical-path velocity (how fast the lane turns a round), the
  confirmation overhead (rounds spent re-measuring near-ties), the
  compute ledger (what each round cost against what it returned), and
  the calibration gap between the predicted and the public score;
- a blended candidate by the ensembling skill's rule: a blend that does
  not beat its members is not an ensemble;
- the ceiling proof: a stop is a decision only with the noise floor, the
  gap and the budget written down;
- the floor: a candidate below the baseline or the board's best from this
  account does not ship.
