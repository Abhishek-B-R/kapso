# Learning

Scope every lesson by the problem's kind (heuristic optimisation, exact,
a machine-learning task under contest rules), the statement's twist and
the data modality, the algorithm family, the input scale and the time
regime. A lesson states the move, structure, allocation or submission
decision that paid or failed and the evidence from the attempts (which
attempt, which seeds or submissions moved, by how much against the
baseline or the board), never this problem's constants, inputs or scores
as facts for the next contest.
