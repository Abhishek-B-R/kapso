# Feedback

Weigh beside the score:

- the cv-protocol skill's reading: a gain inside the measured noise is a tie,
  not a win; where the windowed reading and the flat one disagree, the
  windowed one is the honest one;
- the per-axis frontier: which axes this iteration moved, which are saturated
  with evidence, which the campaign has not tried;
- the feature history: a candidate that widened the matrix with a measured
  reason outranks one that swapped mechanisms at the same score;
- the stage of the budget: a candidate measured on the mini sample is not
  pruned against one measured in full, and when the stage changes the
  scoring changes with it;
- the floor: when the task statement names a baseline to beat (a reproduced
  earlier solution, a published entry), nothing below it ships; a blended
  candidate is judged by the ensembling skill's rule, a meta-model that does
  not beat its members and the plain average is not an ensemble, and an
  ensemble of decorrelated finalists is a candidate in its own right.
