# Feedback

Weigh beside the score:

- the cv-protocol skill's reading: a gain inside the measured noise on
  internal resampling is a tie, not a win; a validation edge the internal
  measurement does not share is suspect; a public measurement, when the
  task has one, is the calibration signal, not the prize;
- the per-axis frontier: which axes this iteration moved, which are saturated
  with evidence, which the campaign has not tried;
- the feature history: a candidate that widened the matrix with a measured
  reason outranks one that swapped mechanisms at the same score, unless
  the wider one costs complexity the gain does not pay for;
- the stage of the budget: a candidate measured on the mini sample is not
  pruned against one measured in full, and when the stage changes the
  scoring changes with it;
- the floor: a baseline the task provides is reproduced under the current
  data before it is trusted, and nothing below the reproduced baseline or
  a published entry the task names ships; a blended candidate is judged by
  the ensembling skill's rule, a blend that does not beat its members and
  the plain mean is not an ensemble, and an ensemble of decorrelated
  finalists is a candidate in its own right.
