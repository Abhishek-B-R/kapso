**Two things to know before you use the score:**
- **It ranks customers rather than giving a churn rate.** `churn_probability` is where a customer falls among the scored population, so it orders customers by risk. The response's `components` field carries the raw per-model probabilities if you need something closer to a rate.
- **It is below the campaign's own goal.** The campaign aimed for AUC above 0.85, and this solution scores 0.845. The deploy shipped it as delivered and did not try to close the gap.
