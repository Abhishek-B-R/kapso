## Proposed harness: `trading`

**Domain:** daily systematic trading strategies. Each one is a `signal(bars)` function that sets a position per instrument from bar data. A protected evaluator scores it on a year that is kept out of the workspace, after transaction costs, and prints the risk figures (drawdown and turnover) beside the score.

**Standing rules** (`instructions/guardrails.md`):
1. A signal at bar *t* uses only bars at or before *t*, and a position decided at *t* earns from *t+1*. *(from the `strategy.py` docstring, the `shift(1)` in `evaluate.py` and the brief's rule)*
2. The contract: `signal(bars)` returns a Series with one value per bar, each in [-1, 1]. Anything else is malformed and scores nothing. *(from `evaluate.py`, exit code 2)*
3. Develop on the development bars only. The out-of-sample year is read by the evaluation alone, never opened, listed or summarised. *(from `generate.py`, `repo/README.md` and the brief)*
4. Every price comes from the pinned snapshot. There are no live fetches and no edits to the bars. *(from the brief and the hashes in `MANIFEST.json`)*
5. The evaluator is never modified. Its `--fast` score is a smoke check and is never quoted as a result. *(from the brief and the `evaluate.py` docstring)*
6. Costs are charged on every change of position, so reducing turnover is part of the strategy. The rate is in the task statement, not in the harness. *(from `evaluate.py`)*

**Candidates, each with its evidence:**

The page tells the verifier to run the two checks itself and quote the output in its verdict. A missing piece of evidence voids the candidate rather than passing it.

1. **Look-ahead scan:** the verifier runs `lookahead-check` on the attempt and quotes its `ok` line. A failure voids the candidate, whatever its score.
2. **Backtest coverage:** the verifier runs `dev-backtest` and checks that its first and last bar match the development span the setup session recorded. A shorter window, or one that runs past it, doesn't count.
3. **Risk line:** the verifier quotes the evaluation's `max_drawdown`, `turnover` and score lines.

I made three changes to support this:
