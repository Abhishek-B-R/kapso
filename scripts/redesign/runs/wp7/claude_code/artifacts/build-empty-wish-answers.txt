This directory doesn't have enough to build on yet. It holds `README.md` ("Our churn modelling work will live here."), a `.env` with only the Claude token, and nothing else. There's no data, evaluator, code, `kapso-config.yaml`, harness or experience source. The README says the domain is churn. The only churn data and evaluator nearby are in the separate sibling project `../churn`:
- `data/train.csv` has 10,000 labelled rows.
- `data/holdout.csv` has 3,000 rows, with their labels kept apart in `private/holdout_labels.csv`.
- `evaluation/evaluate.py` scores ROC AUC.
- The logistic regression shipped there scores 0.797 on those rows.

There were three options; I recommend **A**:
- **A. Build a churn model here and score it with `../churn`'s data and evaluator.** It's measurable right away, and the evaluator meets the contract: the holdout labels sit outside `--data` and are reached through `private_dir.txt`.
- **B. Same model, on your own churn data.** You'd drop a CSV with a label column into `data/`, and I'd build `kapso_evaluation/evaluate.py` from it with a seeded holdout. Nothing can be measured until that data is here.
- **C. Something other than churn.** That needs a one-line wish from you, since nothing here points elsewhere.
