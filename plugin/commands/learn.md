---
description: "Bank what a finished Kapso campaign taught, in a session of its own; then offer to serve the bank"
argument-hint: "[campaign]"
---
Follow the `kapso` skill (`/kapso:kapso`) for: $ARGUMENTS

The campaign is the argument; with none, the newest finished one `kapso watch --all` lists. Launch `kapso learn <path> --detach`, hand off `kapso watch learning/status --follow`, and in the same reply offer the one-line edit (`session_sources: [mine:all]` under `experience:` in `kapso-config.yaml`) that serves the bank to every later session of the project once the learn has banked its cards; do not wait for the learn, which runs for an hour or more. Pass `--config kapso-config.yaml` on every line when the project root holds that file.
