# Feedback

Score against the task statement, not against a generic idea of a good
strategy.

- Ask the risk-reviewer on every evaluated attempt. Its report says whether
  the attempt stayed inside the task's limits (return metric, drawdown,
  exposure, turnover); a broken limit caps the score whatever the return.
- Prefer robust to spectacular: consistency across sub-periods and a flat
  parameter neighbourhood count for more than a higher headline number. The
  risk-limits skill fixes the definitions.
- Say which exploration axis from the ideation page the next attempt should
  move on, and why, with the evidence from this attempt.
- Stop when the goal is met with margin on every limit, not at the first
  attempt that clears the number.
