# Strategy families

| Family | Edge | Fails when | Turnover |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time-series momentum | trends persist over weeks to months | choppy, mean-reverting regimes; sharp reversals | low |
| Cross-sectional momentum | relative winners keep winning | momentum crashes after drawdowns | medium |
| Mean reversion | short-term overreaction | trending markets; after structural breaks | high |
| Carry | paid to hold (funding, roll, yield) | risk-off episodes, crowded unwinds | low |
| Volatility premium | implied exceeds realised | volatility spikes | low to medium |
| Pairs and stat arb | co-moving spreads revert | relationships break | high |

For every proposal, state: the family, the economic rationale (who is on the
other side and why they pay), the regime in which it should lose, and the
expected turnover against the task's cost model. A proposal that cannot name
its losing regime is not ready.
